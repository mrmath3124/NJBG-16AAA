How the rating works

  1. Each game's goal margin counts up to 7 goals either way. Overtime and shootout wins count as one-goal wins.
  2. Game points are the opponent's rating plus that margin.
  3. The rating is the average of the game points: schedule strength plus average goal margin.
  4. Plus-minus is a game's points minus the rating. A win can be negative against a low-rated team, and a loss can be positive against a high-rated one.
  5. MYHockey recalculates every team together on Wednesday mornings, October to April, so the rating can move in a week the team does not play.

The last 10 rating averages the game points of the ten most recent rated games.
